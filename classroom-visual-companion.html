Live voice beta

Works in Chrome on desktop with microphone permission. Chrome sends the audio to Google's speech service to transcribe it. It can't use the mic inside Claude's viewer; download the file and run it from localhost.

Keep it from overloading

          
Busy or off-topic pictures can quietly lower learning, especially for students who struggle to filter distractions. One idea per scene; older scenes fade back after they've had their moment.
