Picture credits

Plato's hand-drawn pictures and drawn diagrams were made for this project. The other pictures come from these open collections, used under their licences. Thank you to everyone who made them.

OpenMoji emoji

The emoji artwork is OpenMoji, the open-source emoji and icon project, used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence. The files in openmoji-bundle.json are OpenMoji SVGs that have been trimmed to save space (comments and unused ids removed, numbers rounded); the drawings themselves are unchanged. That bundle remains available under CC BY-SA 4.0. OpenMoji emoji designs © OpenMoji contributors.

Mulberry Symbols

The symbols in symbols/ (everyday objects, actions, feelings, food, jobs, body parts and country maps) are from Mulberry Symbols version 3.6.1, © 2018–2026 Steve Lee, a symbol set made for people who communicate with pictures. They are used unchanged, except for their file names, under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence, and remain available under it.

US state maps

The state maps in symbols/state-*.svg were drawn for Plato from the US Census Bureau's 2017 cartographic boundary files, which are in the public domain, as redistributed by us-atlas (ISC licence).
